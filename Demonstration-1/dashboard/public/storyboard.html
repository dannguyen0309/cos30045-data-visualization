Demonstration 1 / Labs 1–3 synthesis

From the catalogue to a fair TV comparison

Audience: Australian TV shoppers comparing estimated annual energy use. Snapshot: 4 October 2026. These frames map the existing explorer's reading sequence; sketch bars are layout cues, not numerical findings.

01 / Introduction and filters

What can I explore?

Audience question + snapshotBrand / technology / size / starsFiltered counts and typical energy

Introduce registration records and kWh/year. Let the reader choose a relevant subset. Explain that the same filters update the charts.

02 / Catalogue context

What is represented?

Size registration countsTechnology registration shares

Use counts/shares for catalogue context. Label them as registrations; show historical context as a separately cited source.

03 / Size and energy

How does energy vary with diagonal?

Scatter: inches × annual energyMedian energy by four size bands

Show spread and typical values. State units and explain the median; encourage comparing similar-sized models.

04 / Technology and standby

Is the comparison fair?

Technology × size grouped barsSeparate standby-power histogram

Compare within size bands, keeping colours consistent. Distinguish annual labelled energy from standby watts and disclose missing standby values.

05 / Individual TV comparison

What do these selected models show?

Brand overviewThree similar-sized TV cardsExact diagonals + energy + stars

Move from aggregate context to individual records. Limit the lowest-energy badge to the selected TVs and keep exact size differences visible.

06 / Takeaway and provenance

What should I check next?

Choose size, then compare energy labelsOfficial guidance + source + limits

End with a comparison method, source links and snapshot boundaries. The separate Lab 3 story uses February data and mean energy; this explorer uses October data and medians.

Design: zero-baseline magnitude bars, quantitative position/length channels, nominal technology colours plus text, readable sections and explicit units. Preserve the existing filters and charts while adding narrative context.
